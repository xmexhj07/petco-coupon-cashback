This is the part coupon articles never spell out, and it's where people lose real money. TopCashback's own terms list the situations where cash back fails, and Petco's portal exclusions line up with what most portals report. The offenders:

- **Breaking the click-through chain.** If you click through from TopCashback, then wander off to Google "petco coupon" one more time, or open a bookmarked petco.com link before paying, the retailer credits the last click instead. Same trap if you start on your laptop and pay on your phone.
- **Declining cookies on the consent banner.** Petco's cookie pop-up isn't just a legal formality here — refusing non-essential cookies can sever the tracking.
- **Paying with a gift card or store balance.** Excluded across virtually every portal. Gift card purchases themselves never earn cash back either.
- **Ordering in the Petco app.** Some portals track app purchases, some don't. The browser route is the reliable one.
- **Buying excluded categories.** Gift cards, donations, grooming and training services, and Vital Care membership signups don't earn cash back. Repeat Delivery subscription orders are excluded too — with the exception of your first order on some portals. Taxes and shipping don't count toward the cash back total.
- **Modifying or partially returning the order afterward.** Changes can void the tracking.

None of these are exotic edge cases. Searching for a coupon in a second tab is probably the single most common way people forfeit a 15% rebate on a $100 order — worth $15 to remember.
